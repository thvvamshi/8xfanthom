PHASE 13 — PRODUCT DECISION: MEETING INTENT

============================================================
CONTEXT
============================================================

We are building 8xFathom for the 8x Hiring Software Engineer
take-home assignment.

The existing product already provides:

- Dashboard
- Meetings/history
- Meeting Workspace
- Mocked recording/playback
- Synchronized transcript
- AI summary
- Summary templates
- Action items
- Highlights
- Global search
- Timestamp navigation
- Share view
- Light/dark theme

The product is intentionally a Fathom-style meeting intelligence
workspace, with recording/capture allowed to be stubbed according
to the assignment.

We are now implementing ONE deliberate product decision:

# MEETING INTENT

This is the most important new product capability in the project.

Do NOT treat this as a generic checklist feature.

The product problem is:

Traditional meeting assistants primarily answer:

"What happened in the meeting?"

But a user often enters a meeting with specific outcomes they need:

- confirm a renewal timeline
- understand pricing concerns
- make a decision
- identify an owner
- resolve a blocker
- agree on next steps

A polished AI summary can still fail to answer:

"Did this meeting actually accomplish what I needed?"

Meeting Intent solves that problem.

============================================================
PRODUCT DEFINITION
============================================================

Meeting Intent is:

A small set of user-defined objectives that the product helps
track BEFORE, DURING, and AFTER a meeting.

The product loop is:

BEFORE
Define what matters
        ↓
DURING
Notice what has / has not been covered
        ↓
AFTER
Verify what was achieved
        ↓
RECOVERY
If something remains unresolved, help the user follow up

The product philosophy is:

"Don't wait until the meeting is over to tell me I missed
something important. Help me notice while I can still do
something about it."

This is NOT intended to become a real-time AI meeting assistant
with complex speech processing.

For this assignment, the experience should be deterministic,
seeded, polished, and believable.

============================================================
PRODUCT PRINCIPLE
============================================================

The user remains in control.

The product should NEVER:

- interrupt the meeting automatically
- speak to participants
- automatically ask questions
- automatically schedule meetings
- automatically send messages
- claim a meeting was successful
- make important decisions on behalf of the user

Instead, the product should surface useful information and
give the user an explicit action.

Example:

"You haven't covered renewal timeline yet."

Then:

[Bring this up]

The product suggests a natural question:

"Before we wrap, can we confirm the renewal timeline and
what the next step looks like?"

The user decides whether to use it.

This human-in-the-loop behavior is intentional.

============================================================
THE CORE EXPERIENCE
============================================================

Build the following complete loop:

1. BEFORE MEETING
   User defines 2–5 meeting intents.

2. DURING MEETING
   The Meeting Workspace shows lightweight intent progress.

3. If an important intent is still unresolved,
   surface a subtle "Bring this up" prompt.

4. AFTER MEETING
   Show Meeting Outcome:
   - Covered
   - Partially covered
   - Not covered

5. Each covered/partial result can show evidence:
   - timestamp
   - speaker
   - short transcript quote
   - Open evidence action

6. A missed/unresolved intent can generate a follow-up action.

============================================================
IMPORTANT PRODUCT CONSTRAINT
============================================================

DO NOT make the user constantly interact with Meeting Intent
during the meeting.

This is not a meeting control panel.

The user should primarily focus on the conversation.

Meeting Intent should be:

- visible
- lightweight
- glanceable
- non-intrusive
- actionable only when useful

The existing meeting player/transcript remains the primary
meeting experience.
